PREVIEW
Open index.html in a browser. Images load when the five files are present.
Upload all three code files and the five images to your hosting root.

BUSINESS SETTINGS
Edit SITE_CONFIG at the top of script.js to change phone, weekday hours, and form endpoint.
The structured data and displayed contact links use this configuration.
For consistency without JavaScript, also update the fallback contact text/hours in index.html.
Confirm opening hours with the owner before publication: 9 AM-5 PM weekdays is provisional.
No live open/closed indicator is shown because hours are unconfirmed.

SERVICE FORM
The form is intentionally NOT connected to a mailbox or server.
Until configured, a visible notice explains this and submission does NOT claim success.
Add a real HTTPS endpoint to SITE_CONFIG.formEndpoint to enable delivery.
Configure recipient, spam protection, validation, retention, and notification delivery at that endpoint.
Test a real submission before publishing. A successful request is not an appointment confirmation.
No unverified email address is used and no customer data is stored in browser storage.

LAUNCH CHECKLIST
- Confirm business name, history, services, phone, hours and photo rights with owner.
- Add five actual images; check crops on mobile and desktop.
- Compress photographs and use appropriately sized images (ideally below 250 KB each).
- Test telephone, directions, navigation and form delivery.
- Add your final canonical URL, social sharing image and favicon once domain/assets are confirmed.
- Review any applicable privacy disclosure before enabling form collection.
- Deploy with HTTPS. Test on real mobile devices.

This implementation uses only supplied business content; it adds no reviews, warranties,
certifications, prices, financing, or additional physical locations.
